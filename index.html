01

Scope

This policy applies to the HexoMC Discord verification service. The service lets a player prove ownership of a Minecraft account and connect it to a Discord account for access to verified areas of the HexoMC community.

Linking is optional. It begins only when you request a temporary code in Minecraft and confirm the link through the official HexoMC Discord bot.

02

Data we process

We process only the information needed to operate account verification:

  • Your Discord user ID.
  • Your Minecraft account UUID and current Minecraft name.
  • The date and time the accounts were linked or updated.
  • A temporary cryptographic digest of your verification code.
  • Limited technical logs used to diagnose errors, prevent abuse, and protect the service.

Verification codes are single-use, expire after 15 minutes, and are not stored as readable codes. We do not request or store your Discord password, Minecraft password, private messages, payment information, or advertising profile.

03

How we use data

Verification information is used to:

  • Confirm that you control the Minecraft account being linked.
  • Prevent duplicate links involving the same Minecraft or Discord account.
  • Assign the correct Discord access role and Minecraft nickname.
  • Restore linked status after a bot or network restart.
  • Investigate failures, misuse, and security incidents.

We do not sell personal information or use verification data for targeted advertising.

04

Retention and deletion

Your account link is retained until you unlink it or request its deletion. Expired verification codes are deleted automatically. Operational logs are retained only as long as reasonably needed for reliability, abuse prevention, and security.

/verifyStart account verification
/unlinkDelete the account link

05

Data sharing

Verification information is used by HexoMC systems and authorized operators only. Infrastructure providers may process limited data while hosting the service and database. We do not disclose verification data to data brokers.

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect users, investigate abuse, or defend the security and integrity of HexoMC.

06

Security

HexoMC uses expiring single-use codes, keyed cryptographic digests, access-controlled databases, role-based permissions, and credentials stored outside source control. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but we work to limit access and respond to confirmed incidents.

07

Your choices

You may choose not to link your accounts. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your verification information through the HexoMC ticket system. We may ask you to verify account ownership before completing a request.

08

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the verification service or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published.

09

Support and privacy requests

For privacy questions, account deletion requests, or security reports, join the official HexoMC Discord server and create a private ticket. Do not post personal or account information in public channels.
